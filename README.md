# Keelnat Iyengar Vidhi Companion

An interactive, offline-friendly Nitya & Naimittika ritual companion tailored for **Keelnat (Keezhanatham) Iyengars** (Yajurveda — both **Vadakalai** (Yajurveda Vadakalai Sampradāya) **Thenkalai** Sampradāyas).

## Included Vidhis
1. **Yajurveda Kālatraya Sandhyāvandanam** (Prātaḥ, Mādhyāhnika, Sāyam)
   - All 21 traditional steps with Devanagari, English/IAST transliteration, and Tamil procedural notes
   - Auto-generated personalized **Abhivādanam** (20 Gotrams & Pravarams + Veda/Sūtram)
   - Built-in **Gāyatrī** & **Aṣṭākṣara** Japa counters with optional temple bell chime
2. **Yajurvedīya Darśādi Tarpaṇa Prayogaḥ** *(Keezhanatham Śrī Sudarsana Śarmā Vidhi)*
   - Live substitution of your **Pitṛ Varga** & **Mātāmaha Varga** Gotrams, ancestor names, Mother-living toggle, and Pañcāṅgam details across every mantra
   - Prominent **Poonool position indicators** (`Upavītam` / `Prācīnāvītam` / `Nivītam`)
   - Interactive Tilodakam offering tracker & Sāmagrī checklist
3. **Yajur Veda Upākarma Vidhi (Āvaṇi Aviṭṭam)**
   - **Kāmo'kārṣīt** Japam (108 / 1008 counter, auto-skipped for *Talai Āvaṇi Aviṭṭam*)
   - **Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇam** (Grihastha 2-set vs Brahmachari 1-set + Maunji/Ajina/Daṇḍa)
   - **Nava Kāṇḍa Ṛṣi Tarpaṇam** with Tīrtham hand-posture cues (Ṛṣi, Brāhma, Deva) and 3× counters

## Audio & Chanting Features
- **Built-in Chanting Playback (Web Speech API)**: Listen to any step or individual Tarpaṇam offering in Sanskrit/Devanagari (`sa-IN`/`hi-IN`), Indian English (`en-IN`), or Tamil instructions (`ta-IN`) at adjustable speeds (`0.65×`, `0.80×`, `0.95×`).
- **Authentic Elder / Bruhaspati Recordings**: Record chanting directly in the browser or upload `.mp3`/`.wav`/`.m4a` files for any step — persisted locally in IndexedDB.
- **Tambura Śruti Drone & Japa Chime**: Pure Web Audio API synthesized Sa–Pa–Sa drone and gentle completion bell.

## Running Locally or on GitHub Pages
Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or enable **Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions** after pushing to `main`.
